About

I am an architect based in Madrid, specialising in residential design, from private homes and complex refurbishments to high-end developments. My approach combines spatial clarity, an understanding of how people live and close attention to the details that shape quality—from the organisation of a plan to materials, finishes and everyday comfort.

My experience spans concept design, technical development and construction-stage coordination, working closely with clients, consultants and contractors across Europe and the Americas. I bring a practical understanding of how design decisions affect buildability and property value, with particular strength in identifying a site’s potential and translating a brief into a coherent residential proposal.
