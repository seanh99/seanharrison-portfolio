About

I am an architect and project lead based in Madrid, working at the intersection of architecture, project direction and real estate. My focus is high-end residential and hospitality, leading projects from early concept through technical development, procurement and construction. I am particularly interested in the point where design intent, commercial logic and delivery need to align.

I trained at the Universidad de Buenos Aires and at the École Nationale Supérieure d'Architecture Paris-Malaquais, later completing further studies in Project Management at the Universidad Tecnológica Nacional. My experience includes residential work in Argentina, refurbishment and hospitality projects across Europe, and several years of BIM coordination and construction documentation for civic and educational projects in the United States.

Alongside my professional work, I develop independent architectural studies and collaborations across residential design, hospitality and real estate. These projects allow me to explore architecture not only as a formal discipline, but as a tool for clearer decisions, stronger narratives and long-term value.
