About

I am a design-oriented architect and project lead based in Madrid, working at the intersection of architecture, project direction and real estate. My experience spans residential projects across different scales, from high-end private houses and complex refurbishments to apartment buildings and residential developments. I have worked across early concept, technical development, consultant coordination, procurement and construction, with a particular interest in maintaining design intent through the realities of project delivery.

After several years working across architecture and construction in Buenos Aires, I joined ID Construcciones, a design-build architecture practice where I developed private houses and multi-residential projects through design development, permitting, construction documentation and site delivery. Moving to Europe, I worked on refurbishment projects in Madrid and London, followed by BIM coordination and construction documentation for civic and educational projects in the United States. Since 2024 I have been based in Madrid, leading high-end residential design and project delivery on the owner's side.

Alongside my professional work, I take on selected independent commissions and collaborations across residential design, workplace and real estate development.
