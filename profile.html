About

I am a design-oriented architect and project lead based in Madrid, working at the intersection of architecture, project direction and real estate. My focus is high-end residential and hospitality, leading projects from early concept through technical development, procurement and construction. I am particularly interested in the point where design intent, commercial logic and delivery need to align.

My path began in Buenos Aires, with hands-on design and site experience across residential projects. Moving to Europe, I worked on refurbishment and hospitality projects in Madrid and London, followed by several years of BIM coordination and construction documentation for civic and educational projects in the United States. Since 2024 I have been based in Madrid, leading high-end residential design and project delivery on the owner's side.

Alongside my professional work, I take on independent commissions and collaborations across residential design, hospitality and real estate. These projects allow me to explore architecture not only as a formal discipline, but as a tool for clearer decisions, stronger narratives and long-term value. More recently, I've also been incorporating AI into parts of my process — for project management, 3D modelling and documentation — as another way to work faster without losing precision.
