Canoa 15

I developed the architectural concept and design development for Canoa 15, an 830 m² private residence in Punta Cana conceived as a developer-led property for sale prior to construction. My work established the architectural language, spatial organisation and design definition of the house before the project was handed over to a local architecture practice for permitting and construction documentation.
