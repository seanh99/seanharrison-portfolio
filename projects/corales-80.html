Corales 80

He then developed the complete architectural and interior construction package, including lighting design and landscape concept, while coordinating structural, mechanical and plumbing disciplines with the local contractor. His role also includes procurement, selected FF&E development and ongoing construction support, responding to technical and design queries as the works progress.
