Ramsay 1850

A multi-residential development of eleven apartments developed within ID Construcciones. I joined the project with an initial concept in place, which I revised and developed through structural and MEP coordination, code compliance, permitting and construction documentation. Once construction began, my role extended to site coordination and the integration of purchaser-led modifications into both the architectural package and the ongoing works. The building was completed following my relocation to Madrid, with only minor subsequent design changes.
