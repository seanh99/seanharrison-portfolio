Caledonian Road

Developed for a private investor, the project explored the conversion of four adjoining London townhouses into a coordinated short-stay residential product. The work established the overall spatial strategy, including access, ground-floor and basement extensions, additional mansard accommodation and the internal configuration of the individual units. The architectural proposal was developed alongside the interior concept, material palette and FF&E specification to create a consistent identity across the properties while retaining the scale and character of the existing buildings.

London, United Kingdom · 2022–23
