Maribor Country House

A concept proposal for a private country house in a rural village outside Maribor, Slovenia, exploring how a contemporary residence could sit within a wooded, low-density landscape while retaining the directness of traditional rural architecture.

I developed the architectural concept for this private country house. The project did not proceed, as the client was ultimately unable to acquire the site.
