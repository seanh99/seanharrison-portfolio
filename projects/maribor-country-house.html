Maribor Country House

Concept proposal for a private country house in a rural village outside Maribor, Slovenia. The project explored how a contemporary residence could sit naturally within a wooded, low-density landscape while retaining the directness and solidity associated with traditional rural architecture. The house is conceived as a low, elongated volume organised beneath a simple pitched roof. Rather than treating the landscape as a backdrop, the plan and section are shaped around long views, sheltered outdoor spaces and a continuous relationship between the interior and the garden. A restrained palette of light brick, timber and large glazed openings gives the building a quiet, monolithic character externally, while the interiors become warmer and more tactile through extensive timber surfaces and natural finishes.
