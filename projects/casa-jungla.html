Casa Jungla

Conceived as a precise intervention within a steep tropical landscape, Casa Jungla follows the contours of the mountain through a sequence of long horizontal volumes. Deep concrete slabs, operable timber screens and shaded terraces mediate between domestic life and the surrounding rainforest.

Casa Jungla is an independent private commission. I developed the architectural concept and interior direction, together with a preliminary design sufficient for the client to pursue planning and permitting for the project.
