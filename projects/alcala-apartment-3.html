Alcalá Apartment III

A renovation of a historic Madrid apartment developed at EFC Holdings, combining restored period details with a contemporary material palette across the main living spaces, kitchen and bathrooms.

This project began after I joined EFC Holdings. I followed its development and construction as part of the internal team, providing design management support and selected coordination rather than leading the design from the outset. My involvement centred on following through design decisions already underway and supporting their execution on site.
