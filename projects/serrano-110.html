Serrano 110

The project began as part of the evaluation of a potential building acquisition on Calle Serrano. I developed the initial architectural proposal used to test the property's potential and support the investment decision, after which EFC Holdings proceeded with the acquisition. Since then, I have developed the residential layouts, refurbishment strategy, interior direction and the architectural concept for an approximately 550 m² rooftop extension, together with an alternative scenario should the additional volume not receive approval. The current scheme comprises six residences, including two duplexes, within approximately 4,200 m² of existing building area. I am coordinating the external architecture practice preparing the planning submission and expect to continue directing the design through the subsequent technical stages.
