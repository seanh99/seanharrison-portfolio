BuenBit Offices

A full-building workplace refurbishment for BuenBit in Buenos Aires, developed during 2020 across five levels of an existing building. I developed the architectural concept and construction documentation in collaboration with Follett, reorganising the building around work areas, meeting spaces, shared facilities and social spaces. My involvement also included procurement and coordination of the executive package before handing the project over for construction. The completed workplace remained closely aligned with the architectural strategy developed during this phase, with subsequent interior completion by other teams.

Buenos Aires, Argentina, 2020
Workplace / Office Refurbishment
Developed in collaboration with Follett
Approx. 1,150 m²
