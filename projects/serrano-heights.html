Serrano Heights

Madrid, Spain · 2026
EFC Holdings · concept redesign, architectural renovation and rooftop extension, interior design and design coordination

Set at the highest point of Calle Serrano’s residential stretch, Serrano Heights transforms a historic Madrid building into a collection of contemporary residences. The project combines the restoration of its original architecture with a restrained rooftop extension, refined interiors and shared wellness spaces organized around landscaped courtyards.
