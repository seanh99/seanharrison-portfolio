Casa José Ignacio

A coastal house organised as a sequence of pavilions, courtyards and gardens sheltered from the Atlantic wind. Its architecture combines handcrafted brick walls, deep eaves and transitional spaces.

Casa José Ignacio is an independent private commission. Sean developed the concept and preliminary architectural design to a level sufficient for the client to pursue planning and permitting.
