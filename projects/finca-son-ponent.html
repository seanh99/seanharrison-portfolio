Finca Son Ponent

Developed as part of the evaluation of a potential property acquisition in Mallorca, Finca Son Ponent explores the transformation of an existing historic house through a restrained architectural extension and a new relationship with the surrounding landscape. I developed the architectural concept, initial interior direction and landscape strategy to test the potential of the property before a final acquisition decision.
