Casa Riberas

Casa Riberas is a private residence in the Puertos del Lago development in Buenos Aires, organised as a contemporary family home around a strong relationship between interior spaces, garden and outdoor living areas.

I developed the architectural proposal and municipal approval package for a private house in Buenos Aires Province. The project received municipal approval and was subsequently constructed directly by the clients, while I remained available during the works for occasional design clarifications.
