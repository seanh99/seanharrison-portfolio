Casa Riberas

Commission for a private residence developed from the initial project definition through municipal approval. The work included the overall architectural concept, spatial organisation, material strategy and preparation of the documentation required for approval. Following completion of the design package, the clients appointed their own construction team and managed the delivery of the house independently. The project was conceived as a contemporary family home organised around a strong relationship between interior spaces, garden and outdoor living areas. Large openings, protected terraces and layered façade elements balance openness with privacy, while a restrained material palette gives continuity to the different volumes. The architecture combines clear horizontal planes with vertical timber elements, using changes in depth, transparency and material to articulate the elevations without relying on excessive formal gestures. A complete architectural and municipal approval package was delivered to the clients, who subsequently appointed their own construction team to execute the project. Limited architectural support continued during construction when specific design clarifications or additional details were required.
