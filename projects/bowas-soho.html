Bowas Soho

I was commissioned directly by Bowas to assess the development potential of the site and subsequently developed the architectural proposal through planning. My work covered product definition, apartment layouts, sections, elevations, structural coordination, material strategy and municipal submission documentation. The project progressed into construction based on the development framework established during this phase.
