El Naudir House

El Naudir House is a private lakefront residence within El Naudir, Puertos del Lago, organised around the relationship between the house, its setting and the internal landscape.

El Naudir House is an independent private commission. I developed the site and feasibility studies, the architectural concept and the planning package required for permitting.
