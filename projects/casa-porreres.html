Casa Porreres

Sean took over Casa Porreres with the main structural shell already built and an existing house largely demolished, retaining only selected historic elements including the façade, basement and a stone garden structure. He led the architectural redesign and subsequently developed the project through cost engineering, construction documentation, interior design, MEP, lighting and landscape coordination, supplier procurement and construction. His involvement included regular site visits from Madrid throughout the works, coordination with specialist suppliers and furniture manufacturers, integration of selected pieces from the client's art collection, and final snagging through completion in August 2026.
