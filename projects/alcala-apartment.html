Alcalá Apartment I

A refined renovation of a historic Madrid apartment, where classical proportions are balanced with contemporary interventions, integrated joinery and a restrained palette of natural stone, dark timber and warm neutral finishes.

I joined this project at EFC Holdings after construction had already begun. My contribution was more limited than on other EFC projects, focused on supporting design management, coordinating selected details with suppliers and contractors, and following the project through to delivery during construction.
