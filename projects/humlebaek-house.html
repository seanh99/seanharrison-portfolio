Humlebaek House

Humlebaek House is a family home outside Copenhagen, developed as an independent private commission from initial concept through construction.

I was commissioned shortly after the client acquired the property and led the project from architectural concept through schematic design, design development and construction documentation. My work also included interior coordination, procurement and ongoing construction support. The works were delivered by a trusted local contractor, while I continued to provide remote design direction throughout construction and made site visits in early 2023 and June 2023.
