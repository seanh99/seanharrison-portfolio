CIFO

CIFO is the conversion of an existing Miami warehouse, owned by an art foundation run by EFC Holdings' founders, into a private art archive and gallery with an adjoining retail and commercial space.

Sean developed the spatial strategy and architectural proposal for the conversion of the warehouse, including preliminary coordination with local code requirements. Once the proposal was approved, the project was handed over to a local Miami practice for further development and delivery.
