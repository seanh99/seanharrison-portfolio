Belgrano House

Belgrano House is the renovation of an existing private residence in Belgrano, Buenos Aires, reworked through a restrained contemporary language that balances the character of the original building with a warmer interior atmosphere.

I collaborated with Buenos Aires furniture and interiors studio Melazza on the comprehensive refurbishment of an existing private house in Belgrano. My work focused on the architectural and interior concept, lighting strategy and the design of an upper-floor extension, developed in coordination with Melazza's furniture and interiors work.
