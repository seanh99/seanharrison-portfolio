Montevideo Offices

I collaborated with Buenos Aires furniture and interiors studio Melazza on the conversion of an existing apartment into offices for a law firm in Buenos Aires. My work focused on the architectural and interior concept, reorganising the existing rooms into reception, meeting areas and private offices while preserving the building's original mouldings, herringbone floors and proportions, developed in coordination with Melazza's furniture and interiors work.
